### What the first runs found, and what was done

The first complete run had 14 failures and 14 cells that could not run. None of them was a defect of the E51 or U54 core, and
several were not defects of the runtime at all:

| Finding | Cause | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Every image ended in the trap recorder, no output | OpenOCD of the lab runs on another machine and cannot open the temporary file that `load_image` names; it answers with text, not with an error, and `run_image.py` did not look at the answer | `OCD_ROOT` in `lab.conf`, the answer is checked (`hw/lab.py`, `hw/README.md`) |
| `float_test`, `float_tasking_test`, `float_embedded_test` on the U54: illegal instruction at the first double-precision `fadd`/`fmul` (`mtval` `0x02e7f7d3`, `rm` = 7) | `fcsr` is not 0 after a reset of the silicon (it is on QEMU): with an invalid rounding mode in `frm` every floating-point instruction with the dynamic mode traps | `start.S` writes `fcsr` after it enabled `mstatus.FS` (P1.3); `./x hw reset` and `./x hw initialize` also write 0 to `fcsr` of the four U54 (`hw/hart_state.py`) |
| `textio_*`: "every byte put in loopback mode comes back through Get" | the test set `MCR.LOOP` while the previous line was still in the transmitter; the rest of the line came back as received bytes (QEMU sends at once) | `Console_Checks.Loopback` waits for `LSR.TEMT` first |
| `interrupt_*`: the handler took no byte | (1) the bytes of `console.in` do not exist on the board: the test now feeds them through the MMUART in loopback (`RX_SOURCE=loopback`, `Rx_Source`); (2) the PLIC enables of other contexts survive `./x hw reset` and the previous image, and a context of a halted hart that has the source enabled takes the interrupt away | `./x hw reset` and `./x hw initialize` clear all enables (and `fcsr`, `hw/hart_state.py`), `run_image.py` clears them again before it loads an image; the checks "clear after reset" and "mtimecmp 0 after reset" hold on QEMU only (`Rx_Source.Reset_State_Known`), on the board `mtimecmp` of the other harts must be as at the start |
| `textio_test_e51` and others ran the wrong image | `./x hw run textio_test` took the first `*.elf` of the directory (`console_mmuart1_test`) | `tools/hw.sh` takes the one named like the example; `run_matrix.sh` passes the ELF |
| U54 hart 3 would have run the image of hart 1 | `tools/hw.sh` looked in `bin-u54/` for every U54 hart | looks in `bin-u54-h<N>/` for harts 2 to 4 |
| a run after another one often did not start | a test can leave the board in a state in which no image starts (cause not found: the first failing cell was `trap_embedded_test_u54`, the board was then healthy again only after `./x hw reset`) | `run_matrix.sh` resets and initializes before every cell |
| `itim_reject_test_e51` fails | the test is for a hart whose ITIM is not enabled; the E51's ITIM works in boot mode 0 (`itim_test` passes with `.fast_text` in it), so the image is correctly not rejected | the cell is QEMU only, the runner skips it |

## `delay_accuracy_test` (DELAY_ACCURACY=hardware)

All five harts pass with the limits of `src/hardware/`; the tests report `ok:` for "never early", "at most 2 ticks plus the
latency", "in the past returns at once", "found the hart in `wfi`" and "the hart retires next to no instructions":

| Image (hart) | late min (ticks) | late max, 1 ms | late max, 2 to 50 ms | alarm interrupts found in `wfi` | `minstret` over 100 ms | `mcycle` per tick |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `delay_accuracy_test` E51 (0) | 9 | 13 | 9 | 10 of 10 | 532 | 79 |
| `delay_accuracy_test` U54 (1) | 11 | 15 | 11 | 10 of 10 | 656 | 79 |
| `delay_accuracy_test` U54 (2) | 11 | 15 | 11 | 10 of 10 | 656 | 79 |
| `delay_accuracy_test` U54 (3) | 11 | 15 | 11 | 10 of 10 | 656 | 79 |
| `delay_accuracy_test` U54 (4) | 11 | 14 | 11 | 10 of 10 | 656 | 79 |
| `delay_accuracy_embedded_test` E51 | 9 | 12 | 9 | 10 of 10 | 532 | 79 |
| `delay_accuracy_embedded_test` U54 (1) | 11 | 14 | 11 | 10 of 10 | 656 | 79 |

(1 tick = 1 us. Harts 1 and 3 and the `embedded` images are cells of the table above, harts 2 and 4 were run by hand with
`DELAY_ACCURACY=hardware ./x hw run delay_accuracy_test --hart N --uart-standin`. Earlier runs of the same images gave 14 for
harts 1, 2 and 3 and 13 for the E51: the 1 ms maximum varies by one tick from run to run.)

`Interrupt_Latency_Ticks` was 30 (a placeholder), it is now **18**: the largest deviation of any delay on any hart in any run is 15
ticks (always the 1 ms delay), the smallest 9 (E51) or 11 (U54), so the latency itself is about 13 ticks (2 + 13 = 15); 18 leaves a
margin of 5 ticks. `Max_Late_Ticks` is 20: the hardware bound is 20 us for a 1 ms to 50 ms delay. The issue's guess was 16 to 20.
The measurement is of 50 rounds per interval and about two runs per hart; a longer run could find a later outlier, and then the margin
is the thing to look at.

## The rate of `mtime`

- `mtime` against the host clock (a Python loop reading `CLINT.MTIME` through OpenOCD, half a second apart, 20.3 s in all): **997,252
  ticks per second**; the round trip of the telnet port explains the 0.3 %.
- `mtime` against `mcycle` (`hw/probe_time.S`, hart 1): 75,259 ticks for 6,000,012 cycles, 79.7 cycles per tick (80 MHz CPU clock).
- `SYSREG.RTC_CLOCK_CR` is `0x00010064` after a reset: the clock enable bit is set and `PERIOD` is 100. `./x hw initialize` writes
  `PERIOD` 125. A 125 MHz reference clock gives 1.0 MHz with 125 (measured) and 1.25 MHz with 100; a 100 MHz one would give 0.8 MHz
  with 125. So the reference clock is **125 MHz** and the conflict is solved: `initialize` was right, the `delay_accuracy_test` README
  (100 MHz, `PERIOD` 100) was wrong, and is corrected. `Clock_Frequency` of the runtime (1 MHz) is the measured rate.
- In boot mode 0 `mtime` does not count before the sixth write of `initialize` (`CFG_DDR_SGMII_PHY.CLK_CNTL`), see `icicle.md`.

## LIM and ECC (`hw/probe_ecc.S`, hart 1, board power-cycled before)

Lines at `0x081C0000` that nothing had written since power-up; every access was by a hart, a trap would have been recorded:

| Access | Trap | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `ld` of a never-written dword | no | random data |
| `sb` or `sw` into a never-written dword, then `ld` | no | the bytes written are there, the others are random (`0x14801769 85415a5a` after `sb 0x5a`) |
| `sd`, then `ld` | no | exact |
| `sd` of dword 0, then `ld` of dword 1 or 7 of the same line | no | random data |
| `sd`, then `sb` into it | no | exact |

Consequences for `start.S` and the linker scripts (P1.3, P1.8):

- **ECC is on, it reports, and it never traps.** Reading memory that nothing has written does not trap on the board, in a load or in
  an instruction fetch (see below); the data comes back, and the L2 cache controller (base `0x02010000`) records the event. Measured on
  a power-cycled board with nothing enabled, so ECC needs no activation to count:

  | Register | Offset | What was seen |
  |---|---|---|
  | `DatECCFixAddr` / `DatECCFixCount` | `0x140` / `0x148` | address of the last read of an unwritten line (`0x081C0004`, `0x081C0100`, ...); the count rises by one per read, also for a second read of the same address (no scrubbing) |
  | `DatECCFailAddr` / `DatECCFailCount` | `0x160` / `0x168` | the first read of `0x081C0140` set the count to 1 and the address; the address register holds garbage while the count is 0 |
  | `DirECCFix*`, `DirECCFail*` | `0x100` .. `0x128` | stay 0 (the metadata of the lines is fine) |
  | PLIC pending, `0x0C001000` | | bit 3 (`L2_DATA_CORR`) after the first correctable event, bit 4 (`L2_DATA_UNCORR`) after the first uncorrectable one |
  | `SYSREG.EDAC_SR` | `0x20002100` | stays 0 (it covers the peripheral RAMs, not the L2) |

  The offsets are those of the SiFive L2 cache controller; the address registers matched the addresses read, so they are right.
  A read of a never-written dword is a single-bit (corrected, `DatECCFixCount`) or a double-bit (`DatECCFailCount`) error; both return
  data (the same value every time for a given dword) and neither raises a bus error. `probe_ecc.S` alone counted 54 correctable
  and 56 uncorrectable events across its seven tests; which access caused which event is in the part 3 probe below.
- **Enabling the interrupt (`hw/probe_ecc_irq.S`, hart 1):** with priority 1, the enables of sources 1 to 4 in the context of hart 1
  and `mie.MEIE`, the pending sources 3 and 4 were taken at once (claim ids 3, then 4). After that the hart took about 61,000
  further external interrupts in a few seconds, the claim ids of which I did not record, so whether a source stays asserted until a
  count register is read is **not known**. The consequence: an image must not enable PLIC sources 1 to 4, and none of the runtime
  profiles does (`./x hw reset` and `run_image.py` clear all enables).
- **A fetch from a never-written line does not fault either:** `jalr` into `0x081C2000` ended in an illegal instruction (`mcause` 2),
  `mtval` `0x3214`, i.e. the garbage was fetched and decoded. The `mcause` 1 of `run_image.py`'s docstring (partly written lines of an
  image) was not reproduced, see the next item.
- **Which access raises an ECC event, and a fetch from a line written in part (`hw/probe_ecc_rmw.S`, hart 1).** One fresh 64-byte
  line per test, the two counters read before and after the one access (one run, one board; the counters do not start at 0 because
  loading the probe through OpenOCD already causes events):

  | Access to a never-written line | `DatECCFixCount` | `DatECCFailCount` | Trap |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | `sb` into a dword | +0 | +1 | no |
  | `sw` into a dword | +1 | +0 | no |
  | `sd` into a dword | +1 | +0 | no |
  | `sd` into dword 0, then `sd` into dword 1 of the line | +1 for the first, +1 for the second | +0 | no |
  | 8 `sd`, a whole line | +4 | +4 | no |
  | `ld` of a dword | +1 | +0 | no |
  | `jalr` into a line of which two dwords (`addi; ret`) were written, then `fence.i` | +2 | +1 | no, the code ran |
  | `jalr` into a line written as a whole (8 `sd`), then `fence.i` | +0 | +0 | no, the code ran |

  So a full 64-bit store raises events as well; the counters do not tell a narrow from a wide store, and a store to a fresh line
  is not free of ECC events whatever its width. Whether the L2 reads the line on a store, or counts something else, is not known.
  The fetch from a line written in part did not fault (the earlier `mcause` 1 was not reproduced, with `fence.i` before the jump
  and a line that was written from the start of the line); a fetch from a line that is written in another order, or without the
  `fence.i`, was not tried.

Consequences for `start.S` and the linker scripts (P1.3, P1.8):

- The start code needs to zero `.bss` and the stacks as it does, and nothing more for the safety of the start; `.noinit` may hold
  anything. `heap_test` (the TLSF heap, which writes its structures before it reads them) and `exceptions_test` pass on the board.
  A program that reads LIM it has not written gets garbage and an ECC event, not a trap, so the only visible sign is the counters.
- **The 64-bit-only store rule did not matter for the data in these runs:** narrow stores did not trap, and the bytes written were
  read back; the bytes of the dword that a narrower store leaves unwritten are random. It also did not matter for a fetch in the
  one case tried (a line written in part). `run_image.py` keeps writing whole lines because that is safe and costs nothing, not
  because this work showed that it is needed. One board and one run per case.
- No change of `start.S` or the linker scripts for ECC was needed. The only change of `start.S` is the `fcsr` write above.
- Not tried: the ECC error injection register (`0x040`), the effect of the L2 interrupts at the PLIC of other harts, and a fetch from a
  line that was written in part without `fence.i`.

## The L2 scratchpad (`--memory scratchpad`, `hw/l2_scratchpad.py`)

Boot mode 0 runs no boot software, so nothing enables the cache ways that an image of the scratchpad placement runs in
(`place-scratchpad.ld`: `0x0A000000`, 2 ways of 128 KB in the default partition). The own MSS initialization of `./x hw initialize`
does not either. `hw/l2_scratchpad.py` does it through OpenOCD, in the order of `config_l2_cache` of the platform HAL
([`mss_l2_cache.c`](https://github.com/polarfire-soc/platform/blob/master/mpfs_hal/common/mss_l2_cache.c), registers in
`mss_l2_cache.h`): `WayEnable` (`0x02010008`) = cache ways + scratchpad ways - 1, all 15 `WayMask` registers (`0x02010800` ..
`0x02010870`) = the cache ways, then for each scratchpad way the mask of the E51 D cache set to that way and its 128 KB of the zero
device written. After a reset `WayEnable` is 0 and every mask `0xFFFF`.

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| after the set-up, a 64-bit pattern at the start and the end of each way | read back exactly, the two ways independent (`0x0A000000`, `0x0A01FFF8`, `0x0A020000`, `0x0A03FFF8`) |
| the LIM after `WayEnable` = 2 | 13 ways (`0x08000000` .. `0x081A0000`); the images and the trap recorder (`0x08100000`) are below that |
| images of the scratchpad on the board (`reset`, `initialize --minimal`, `l2_scratchpad.py`, `./x hw run`, one set-up per cell) | `trap_test`, `elab_test`, `tasking_test`, `exceptions_test`, `secstack_test` on the E51 and the U54, `tasking_embedded_test`, `float_test`, `float_tasking_test`, `protected_test`, `interrupt_test` (loopback) and `textio_test` on the U54: 16 cells, all pass, the code runs from `0xA0xxxxx` (the `loaded ... bytes at 0xa000000` line of each log) |
| `report_test` | its image is pinned to the LIM (`loaded ... at 0x8000000`), so those two passes say nothing about the scratchpad |
| `heap_test` | not applicable: it requires a heap of 512 KiB, the scratchpad leaves 110,992 bytes (`FAIL: the heap is at least 512 KiB`) |

- **The end of a primary image resets the MSS** (`__gnat_exit` in `start.S` writes `MSS_RESET_CR`, also after a fatal error), and that
  undoes the set-up: `WayEnable` 0, masks `0xFFFF`, `MTIME` 0. A scratchpad image that is started after that still finds its first
  128 KB in way 0 (the zero device is addressed) and runs until something evicts its lines. That is what the first runs looked like:
  a run of `exceptions_test` on the U54 ended in an illegal instruction with `mtval` 0 at a `jal` of the image, twice in a row, and
  afterwards 27 lines of the code at the start of the image read as zeros while the lines of `.data` held data; at the end of the
  next run `WayEnable` read 0 and `MTIME` 0. With the set-up before every cell (`tests/hw/run_matrix.sh -m scratchpad`) the same
  images pass. I did not prove that the eviction was the cause of each early failure: the evidence is the register state and the
  zeroed lines, and that the failure went away with the set-up.
- `./x hw run` neither sets up the L2 nor resets: run `./x hw reset`, `./x hw initialize --minimal` and `hw/l2_scratchpad.py` before each
  image of the scratchpad; the set-up takes about 40 s (`mwd ADDR 0 COUNT` through OpenOCD).
- Not run from the scratchpad: the images of `itim_test`, `delay_accuracy_test`, the `console_*` variants and the others of the list.
  The U54 harts 2 to 4 and the secondary role (`MPFS_BOOT_ROLE=secondary`) were not tried either. One board, one set-up per cell.

## The console without the UART stand-in (issue #50, 2026-11-11)

The runs of the matrix used `--uart-standin`. These ran with `--uart-clocks`, which only releases the clock and the reset of the
MMUARTs: the divisor, the fractional divisor and the line format are the runtime's own (`__mpfs_console_init = 1`). Each image ran
after `./x hw reset` and `./x hw initialize --minimal`, the serial ports are `/dev/ttyUSB0` to `/dev/ttyUSB3` (`UART_PATTERN`), one board.
